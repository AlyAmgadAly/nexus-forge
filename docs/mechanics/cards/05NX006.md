## 4. Clause details

### C1 — Discard 1 to play me

| Field | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| **Trigger** | Playing this card from hand. Not summon, revive, copy, or put into play. |
| **Timing** | During play-cost payment, before the unit enters play and before its play/summon triggers. No priority window. |
| **Condition** | The controller's hand contains at least one card other than Reborn Grenadier. |
| **Cost** | Discard exactly one card from the controller's hand. The mana cost is 1. |
| **Target** | One card in the controller's own hand, excluding the card being played. |
| **Illegal targets** | Reborn Grenadier itself; the partner's hand in 2v2; cards in the deck, discard pile, or on the board. |
| **Board state** | A full board before the play rejects it: the discard cost frees no board slot. Grenadier reserves one slot; if the discard trigger's copy would take that slot, the copy's summon fails and the discard remains (R-004, R-033). |
| **Choice** | The controller chooses. Mandatory. The play cannot be confirmed without a legal choice. |
| **Cancellable** | Yes, before payment. Cancellation is a no-op. |
| **Action** | Discard the chosen card. It is a real discard, not a burn or a move to another zone. |
| **Duration** | Immediate. The discard is permanent. |
| **Zone** | The discarded card moves from hand to its owner's discard pile. Grenadier moves from hand to the board. |
| **Owner** | The controller pays the cost from their own hand. The discarded card's owner is the controller. |
| **Ordering** | Validate (including an open board slot) -> pay 1 mana -> reserve one board slot for Grenadier -> discard the chosen card -> resolve the discard trigger -> put Grenadier into play -> fire Grenadier's play/summon triggers. |
| **Prevention** | Not applicable; a discard cost is not damage, a spell, or a skill. |
| **Source / attribution** | The controller discards; the discarded card's own trigger uses the card as its source. |
| **Mandatory** | Yes. If the hand has no other card, the card cannot be played. |
| **Repeatable** | Once per play. If Grenadier is returned to hand and played again, the cost is paid again. |
| **Logging** | The discarded card is public: both players may see which card was discarded. Do not reveal the rest of the hand. |

**What must not happen:**

- The cost must not discard Reborn Grenadier itself.
- The cost must not discard a random card or the first card without a choice.
- The cost must not discard from the partner's hand in 2v2.
- The cost must not discard from the deck or the discard pile.
- The cost must not fire the discard trigger twice.
- The cost must not summon Grenadier before the discard trigger resolves.
- A rejected or cancelled play must not change mana, hand, board, discard, or
  stats.
- The player must not be able to pay the cost without enough mana.
- Hand-limit burn must not satisfy or trigger this clause.
- The play must not succeed when the board is full; the discard cost frees no
  board slot.
- A discard-triggered copy must not take Grenadier's reserved slot; if it would,
  the copy's summon fails and the discard remains.

**Engine mapping:**

- Existing DSL vocabulary: `discard` action, `discardCards()` helper, `discard`
  event, hand-card targets.
- Missing vocabulary: a pre-play cost stage and a specific-card discard choice.
  The current `discardCards()` selects randomly or takes the first card.
- Reuses primitive: [additional-play-cost.md](../families/additional-play-cost.md).
- Proposed script shape:

```json
{
  "playCost": {
    "type": "discard",
    "amount": 1,
    "selection": "choose",
    "excludeSelf": true
  }
}
```

> The script shape is an implementation proposal, not a ruling. The implementer
> may choose a different shape if it preserves the behavior and passes lint.

**Current engine path to change:**

- `Game.discardCards()` at `server/engine/game.js:1156` accepts `count`, `random`,
  `source`, and `reason`. It picks a random index or index 0. It stores only
  `h.cardId` in the discard pile. It emits the discarded card's own `discard`
  trigger with a virtual source that has `handCard: true`, then emits watchers.
- The play cost needs a specific hand entry (`iid`) and must exclude the card
  being played.
- The play cost needs a hand-entry snapshot for C2, because the discard pile
  stores only card IDs.
- `Game.playCard()` pays mana, removes the card from hand, and summons before
  play triggers; the discard must happen earlier, in a new cost stage.

### C2 — When I'm discarded, summon an exact copy

| Field | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| **Trigger** | This card is discarded from hand. |
| **Timing** | Immediately after the discard event, before the card or effect that caused the discard enters play or resolves. |
| **Condition** | The discard came from hand. Not hand-limit burn, not a deck mill, not a death, not a transform, not a move to another zone. |
| **Cost** | None. The trigger is free. |
| **Target** | This card (the discarded card). |
| **Action** | Summon an exact copy of the discarded card. |
| **Duration** | Permanent until the copy leaves play. The copy is Ephemeral because the original is Ephemeral. |
| **Zone** | The discarded card is in the discard pile; the copy is summoned to its owner's board. |
| **Owner** | The owner of the discarded card, not the player who caused the discard. |
| **Ordering** | The discard event creates the trigger; the trigger resolves before the causing card enters play or resolves. The copy's summon triggers and summon watchers fire before the causing card's play/summon triggers. |
| **Prevention** | A full board can prevent the summon (R-033). A game-over state prevents it. |
| **Source / attribution** | The discarded card is the source of its own trigger. The trigger is mandatory. |
| **Mandatory** | Yes. It is not an optional "you may." |
| **Repeatable** | Every time this card is discarded from hand. A copy that is later returned to hand and discarded triggers again. |
| **Logging** | The summon is public. Log the copy's stats and Ephemeral keyword. Do not reveal hidden hand contents beyond the discarded card. |

**What must not happen:**

- The trigger must not fire on hand-limit burn.
- The trigger must not fire on a deck mill, obliterate, death, transform, recall,
  capture, or move to deck.
- The trigger must not fire when the card is played or summoned.
- The trigger must not pay the mana or discard cost.
- The trigger must not fire the copy's play triggers; it is a summon.
- The trigger must not summon the copy for the player who caused the discard
  instead of the owner.
- The trigger must not summon twice for one discard.
- The trigger must not copy damage or equipment that the hand card did not have.
- The trigger must not create an infinite loop from its own discard.
- The trigger must not fire if the card was discarded from hand but the discard
  was actually a burn (see R-023).
- The trigger must not leave the copy on the board if the game is over.

**Engine mapping:**

- The existing `discardCards()` already detects `scriptHasEvent(card.script,
  EVENTS.DISCARD)` and creates a virtual source. That is the right hook.
- The virtual source currently carries `card`, `owner`, `team`, `isSpell`, and
  `handCard: true`. It does not carry the hand entry's buffs, keywords, or
  fleeting state. C2 needs those to make an exact copy.
- `Game.makeUnit()` at `server/engine/game.js:230` already applies
  `handEntry.buffs` and `handEntry.keywords`, including filtering round-duration
  buffs to the current round. It can build the copy if the hand entry is
  preserved.
- `Game.summonUnit()` at `server/engine/game.js:1649` accepts `handEntry` and
  `source`. The trigger can call it with the preserved entry and the owner.
- The trigger should use the owner's player object, not the discarding player.
- If the board is full, `summonUnit()` returns null and logs; the discard remains.
  R-033 is confirmed: the copy is not created, fires no summon triggers, and is
  not moved to hand or removed from the discard pile.

**Current discard-related engine paths:**

| Path | Current behavior | Consequence for C2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `discardCards()` (`game.js:1156`) | Random or first card; stores card IDs; emits self trigger + watchers. | Needs a specific-card choice and a hand-entry snapshot. |
| `drawCard()` (`game.js:1095`) | Hand-limit burn logs `burn` and returns; no discard event. | Burn does not trigger C2. Good. |
| `addCardToHand()` (`game.js:1113`) | Hand-limit burn logs `burn` and returns; no discard event. | Burn does not trigger C2. Good. |
| `endRound()` (`game.js:1409`) | Fleeting cards are removed and logged as `discard`, but no discard event is emitted. Ephemeral deaths happen earlier, at `game.js:1400`. | R-024 confirmed: Fleeting discard triggers C2; resolve discard triggers first, then Ephemeral deaths, so the copy dies in the same cleanup. The engine must emit the event and use that order. |
| `makeUnit()` (`game.js:230`) | Applies hand-entry buffs and keywords. | Reusable for the exact copy. |
| `summonUnit()` (`game.js:1649`) | Accepts `handEntry`; fires summon triggers and watchers. | Reusable for C2; it is a summon, not a play. |

## 7. Golden tests

### G1 — Basic play with a discard

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `05NX006`, `X` (a vanilla card)
- P1 board: empty
- P1 mana: 1
- P2 board: empty
- Phase: playing; P1 has priority; round 1.

**Action:** P1 plays `05NX006` and chooses `X` to discard.

**Expected:**

- `X` leaves hand and is in P1's discard pile.
- `05NX006` is on P1's board as a 3/2 Ephemeral unit.
- P1 hand is empty; P1 mana is 0.
- The discard trigger does not fire for `X` unless `X` has its own discard
  trigger.
- Log order: discard `X` -> summon Reborn Grenadier.
- No play/summon trigger fires twice.

**Why this test exists:** Baseline cost and entry.

### G2 — Hand contains only Grenadier

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `05NX006`
- P1 mana: 1
- Phase: playing; P1 has priority.

**Action:** P1 attempts to play `05NX006`.

**Expected:**

- Play is rejected with a deterministic needs-cost error.
- `05NX006` stays in hand; P1 mana stays 1.
- No discard, no summon, no stats increment.

**Why this test exists:** The card cannot discard itself.

### G3 — Self-discard is illegal

**Setup:** P1 hand `05NX006`, `X`; P1 mana 1.

**Action:** P1 attempts to play `05NX006` and chooses `05NX006` itself as the
discard.

**Expected:**

- The choice is rejected.
- `05NX006` stays in hand; `X` stays in hand; mana stays 1.
- No copy is summoned.
- No infinite loop.

**Why this test exists:** Self-discard must be excluded before payment.

### G4 — Opponent discards your Grenadier

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `05NX006`
- P1 board: empty
- P2 has an effect that discards a card from P1's hand.
- P2 controls the effect.

**Action:** P2 resolves the discard effect and chooses `05NX006`.

**Expected:**

- `05NX006` goes to P1's discard pile.
- P1 summons a 3/2 Ephemeral copy on P1's board.
- P2 does not get the copy.
- The copy's summon triggers fire once.
- P1's hand is empty.

**Why this test exists:** Copy ownership follows the card owner.

### G5 — Discard another Grenadier

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `05NX006` (G1), `05NX006` (G2)
- P1 mana: 1
- P1 board: empty.

**Action:** P1 plays G1 and discards G2.

**Expected:**

- G2 is discarded; its C2 triggers and summons a copy C.
- Then G1 is summoned.
- P1 has two 3/2 Ephemeral Grenadiers: C and G1.
- Both are on the board; both are Ephemeral.
- The log order is G2 discard -> C summon -> G1 summon.
- No copy pays the discard cost.

**Why this test exists:** Discard trigger resolves before the played card enters
play; multiple bodies are correct.

### G6 — Salt and Stitches interaction

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `05NX006`, `05NX003` Salt And Stitches
- P1 mana: 2 (enough for Salt)
- P1 board: empty.

**Action:** P1 plays Salt And Stitches and discards `05NX006`.

**Expected:**

- `05NX006` is discarded; C2 summons copy C (3/2 Ephemeral).
- Salt And Stitches then summons a second Reborn Grenadier S and gives S +2|+0
  this round.
- C is 3/2; S is 5/2 this round.
- Both are Ephemeral.
- The spell's buff does not apply to C.

**Why this test exists:** The discard trigger resolves before the spell's own
summon, and the spell's "it" refers to the unit it summoned.

**Open:** Confirm this with the oracle before implementing Salt And Stitches.

### G7 — Hand-limit burn does not trigger

**Setup:**

- P1 hand is at the limit (10).
- P1 has an effect that draws a card, and the top card is `05NX006`.

### G8 — Fleeting round-end discard

### G12 — Multiple discards

**Setup:** P1 hand contains two `05NX006` cards. An effect discards both at once.

**Action:** Resolve the discard.

**Expected:**

- Two discard events resolve sequentially in the order discarded.
- Each Grenadier summons a copy; if the board can hold both, both are summoned.
- If the board fills, the later summon fails and that discarded card remains in
  the discard pile.
- The log records the discard order and each summon or failure.
- No copy is summoned twice.

**Why this test exists:** Multiple discards resolve sequentially, not as one
simultaneous batch (R-026).

**Why this test exists:** Ephemeral is a copied keyword, and death is not discard.

### G17 — Discard trigger competes with the played Grenadier's slot

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `05NX006` (G1), `05NX006` (G2)
- P1 board: 5 other units (5 of 6)
- P1 mana: 1.

**Action:** P1 plays G1 and discards G2.

**Expected:**

- The board is at 5 of 6 before the play; the discard frees no slot.
- G1 reserves the sixth slot.
- G2's C2 triggers, but its copy would take G1's reserved slot, so that summon

- G1 enters play as the sixth unit.
- Only one Grenadier (G1) is on the board.
- The log records G2's discard, the failed copy summon, then G1's summon.

**Why this test exists:** A cost-triggered copy cannot take the played card's
reserved slot (R-004, R-033).
