## 4. Clause details

### C1 — Kill an ally to play me

| Field | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| **Trigger** | Playing this card from hand. Not summon, revive, copy, or put into play. |
| **Timing** | During play-cost payment, before the unit enters play and before its play/summon triggers. No priority window. |
| **Condition** | None beyond a legal allied unit. |
| **Cost** | Kill one allied unit. The mana cost is 0. |
| **Target** | One allied unit on the controller's team. |
| **Illegal targets** | Enemy units, landmarks, equipment, spells, cards in hand/deck/discard, the card being played, and units protected by "cannot die"/"unkillable" effects. |
| **Choice** | The controller chooses. Mandatory. The play cannot be confirmed without a legal choice. |
| **Cancellable** | Yes, before payment. Cancellation is a no-op. |
| **Action** | Kill the chosen unit. It is a real death, not damage and not obliteration. |
| **Duration** | Immediate. The death is permanent. |
| **Zone** | The killed unit moves from the board to its owner's discard pile. Butcher moves from hand to the board. |
| **Owner** | The controller pays the cost. The killed unit's owner is whoever owns it; in 2v2 that may be the partner. |
| **Ordering** | Validate -> pay mana (0) -> kill the ally -> resolve death/Last Breath/watcher events -> put Butcher into play -> fire Butcher's play/summon triggers. |
| **Prevention** | Barrier, SpellShield, Tough, and Ephemeral do not prevent a kill. Only an explicit "cannot die"/"unkillable" effect does. |
| **Source / attribution** | The cost has no unit source. Butcher is not in play and is not the killer. See R-012. |
| **Mandatory** | Yes. If no legal ally exists, the card cannot be played. |
| **Repeatable** | Once per play. If Butcher is returned to hand and played again, the cost is paid again. |
| **Logging** | The kill is public. Log the chosen ally and the death. Do not log hidden hand contents. |

**What must not happen:**

- The play must not succeed with no allied unit.
- The cost must not kill an enemy unit or a landmark.
- The cost must not deal damage instead of killing.
- The cost must not resolve after Butcher is already on the board.
- The opponent must not get a priority window to respond to the cost.
- A rejected or cancelled play must not change mana, hand, board, discard, or
  stats.
- The killed unit's death trigger must not fire twice.
- Butcher must not be summoned if the game ends during cost events.

**Engine mapping:**

- Existing DSL vocabulary: `kill` action, `allyUnit` target, death/Last Breath
  events, `killUnit()` and `emitDeaths()`.
- Missing vocabulary: a pre-play cost stage. A normal `play` trigger is too late:
  `playCard()` pays mana, removes the card from hand, and calls `summonUnit()`
  before `runScript(..., PLAY)` fires.
- Reuses primitive: [additional-play-cost.md](../families/additional-play-cost.md).
- Proposed script shape:

```json
{
  "playCost": {
    "type": "killAlly",
    "target": { "type": "allyUnit", "count": 1 }
  }
}
```

> The script shape is an implementation proposal, not a ruling. The implementer
> may choose a different shape if it preserves the behavior and passes lint.

**Current engine path to change:**

- `Game.canPlay()` does not check additional-cost legality.
- `Game.playCard()` validates only script targets, pays mana, removes the card
  from hand, and summons before play triggers.
- `Game.handView()` and `Game.playable()` do not know about the cost.
- `Game.killUnit()` is the correct death helper; call it before `summonUnit()`.
- `Game.emitDeaths()` already resolves the dying unit's own trigger before allied
  watchers.

## 7. Golden tests

### G1 — Basic play

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `01SI011`
- P1 board: `A` (2/2 vanilla)
- P1 mana: 0
- P2 board: `B` (2/2 vanilla)
- Phase: playing; P1 has priority; round 1.

**Action:** P1 plays `01SI011` and selects `A`.

**Expected:**

- `A` leaves play and is in P1's discard pile.
- `01SI011` is on P1's board as a 3/2 unit.
- P1 hand is empty; P1 mana is 0.
- P2 board is unchanged; P2 Nexus is unchanged.
- Log order: `A dies.` -> `P1 summons Ravenous Butcher (3|2).`
- No play/summon trigger fires twice.

**Why this test exists:** Baseline cost, death, and summon order.

---

### G2 — No legal ally

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `01SI011`
- P1 board: empty
- P2 board: `B` (2/2 vanilla)
- P1 mana: 0
- Phase: playing; P1 has priority.

**Action:** P1 attempts to play `01SI011`.

**Expected:**

- Play is rejected with a deterministic needs-cost/illegal-cost error.
- `01SI011` stays in hand.
- P1 mana stays 0; P1 board stays empty.
- `B` is untouched; no death trigger fires.
- No stats increment.

**Why this test exists:** The cost is mandatory; an enemy unit is not an ally.

---

### G3 — Only a landmark

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `01SI011`
- P1 board: an allied landmark
- P2 board: empty
- P1 mana: 0.

**Action:** P1 attempts to play `01SI011`.

**Expected:**

- Play is rejected.
- The landmark is not killed.
- The card stays in hand; no mana is spent.

**Why this test exists:** "Ally" means a unit, not any allied card.

---

### G4 — Last Breath ordering

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `01SI011`
- P1 board: `A` with "Last Breath: draw 1" and a known top card
- P1 deck: at least 2 cards
- P1 mana: 0.

**Action:** P1 plays `01SI011`, selects `A`.

**Expected:**

- `A` dies; its Last Breath draws 1 before Butcher is summoned.
- The drawn card is in hand when Butcher enters play.
- Butcher is on the board as 3/2.
- The log shows `A dies.` -> Last Breath draw -> Butcher summon.
- The draw does not fire twice.

**Why this test exists:** Cost events resolve before the played unit's entry.

---

### G5 — Cancel before payment

**Setup:** P1 hand `01SI011`; P1 board `A`; P1 mana 0.

**Action:** P1 starts the play, selects `A`, then cancels before confirming.

**Expected:**

- `A` is alive and unchanged.
- `01SI011` is still in hand.
- Mana, board, discard, and stats are unchanged.
- No death or summon trigger fires.
- No pending choice remains.

**Why this test exists:** Cancellation must be a no-op.

---

### G6 — Barrier, SpellShield, and champion targets

Run as separate controlled cases with the same setup as G1, replacing `A` with:

- a unit with Barrier;
- a unit with SpellShield;
- a champion.

**Expected:** all three are legal targets and die. Barrier and SpellShield do not
prevent a kill cost. The champion follows the normal champion death rules.

**Why this test exists:** Kill is not damage and not an enemy spell.

---

### G7 — Board filled by the cost trigger

**Setup:**

- P1 hand: `01SI011`
- P1 board: `A`, whose Last Breath summons a unit, with the team board at 5 of 6
- P1 mana: 0.

**Action:** P1 plays `01SI011`, selects `A`.

**Expected:** **to be filled after R-004/R-014.** The proposed rule is that Butcher
reserves the sixth slot, the Last Breath summon fails or uses another slot, and
Butcher enters play. The test must assert the chosen rule, not an accidental
engine outcome.

**Why this test exists:** Atomic payment and board-slot reservation.

---

### G8 — 2v2 partner unit

**Setup:** P1 and P2 are teammates; P1 hand `01SI011`; P2 board `A`; P1 mana 0.

**Action:** P1 plays `01SI011` and selects P2's `A`.

**Expected:** `A` dies; P2's death triggers fire; Butcher is on the team board.
The enemy team's units are not legal targets.

**Why this test exists:** Team-scoped "ally" in 2v2.

---

### G9 — Summoned, not played

**Setup:** P1 has a revive or summon effect that puts `01SI011` into play; P1 has
no other allies; P1 mana is sufficient for the effect.

**Action:** Resolve the summon/revive.

**Expected:** Butcher enters play without killing an ally and without paying the
additional cost. Only the summon trigger path fires.

**Why this test exists:** "To play" applies only to a play from hand.

---

### G10 — Two Butchers

**Setup:** P1 has `01SI011` on the board and a second `01SI011` in hand; P1 mana 0.

**Action:** P1 plays the second Butcher and selects the first.

**Expected:** The first Butcher dies and goes to the discard pile; the second
enters play as 3/2. The first's death does not trigger a cost.

**Why this test exists:** A previously played Butcher is a legal ally.

---

### G11 — Hand-limit protection

**Setup:** P1 hand is at the limit; P1 plays `01SI011`; the killed ally's Last
Breath draws 1.

**Action:** Resolve the play.

**Expected:** **to be filled after R-004.** Proposed: Butcher is not burned; the
drawn card is burned or handled by the hand-limit rule. The play still resolves.

**Why this test exists:** Cost-triggered draws must not consume the card being
played.

---

### G12 — Game over during the cost

**Setup:** P1 plays `01SI011`; the killed ally's Last Breath deals lethal to the
enemy Nexus or otherwise ends the game.

**Action:** Resolve the cost.

**Expected:** **to be filled after R-014.** Proposed: the game ends; Butcher does
not enter play; the winner is correct; no post-game action is accepted.

**Why this test exists:** Terminal-state handling during cost events.
